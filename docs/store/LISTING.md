## Full description (2101/4000)

```
Criczy brings every cricket match to your phone: live scores, ball-by-ball commentary and a replay of every shot, in a clean, fast app made for fans.

LIVE SCORES
• Tests, ODIs, T20s and domestic cricket: live, upcoming and finished
• Filter by international, format or women's cricket, or show only the teams you follow
• Pin a live match to keep its score in your notification bar while play is on
• Three home screen widgets: just the score, the score with the match situation, or a match centre with the batters, the bowler and this over

MATCH CENTRE
• The chase at a glance: target, runs needed and run rates
• Who is at the crease and who is bowling, with the partnership and the last wicket
• Full scorecards for every innings, with extras and fall of wickets
• Squads by role, a side-by-side player comparison, venue and officials
• Share a scorecard as an image

BALL BY BALL
• Commentary on every ball, with wickets and boundaries a tap away
• See shot: replay any ball from the bowler's hand to where it went, drawn on the ground
• Replay a whole over, ball by ball

ANALYSIS
• Worm and Manhattan charts, wagon wheels and pitch maps
• Partnerships, phases of the innings, and how each batter scored against pace and spin

FOLLOW YOUR TEAMS
• Follow up to five teams, plus series and single matches
• Alerts when a followed match is about to start, gets under way, loses a wicket or finishes
• Team pages with recent form, player profiles, and series tables with tournament stats
• Cricket news, filtered to the teams you follow
• Search for teams, series, matches and players

FAN COMMUNITY
• Join with just a username: no email, phone number or password
• Share opinions and run polls in your team's feed, and vote "Who wins?" on match days
• Posts disappear after 24 hours, and anything abusive can be reported or blocked

MADE FOR EVERY DAY
• No account needed to follow scores
• Works offline with the last scores it loaded
• Dark and light themes, and a layout made for tablets

Criczy is an independent app. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by any cricket board, league or team.
```
