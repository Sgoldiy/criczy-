# Criczy privacy policy

_Last updated: 17 November 2026_

## Summary

Criczy does not ask you to sign in, does not collect personal information, and contains no advertising or analytics software.

## What stays on your device

Your favourites, settings, the list of players you have come across, and a cache of scores so the app works offline. These never leave your device, and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## What is sent over the internet

To show scores, news and images the app requests them from cricket.com.au's servers. As with any website, those servers receive your IP address and basic technical details of the request. Criczy adds no identifiers to these requests.

## Notifications

Match alerts are worked out on your device by checking scores periodically. No notification service or token is involved. You can turn alerts off in More, or in your system settings.

## Links

News stories, videos, tickets and maps open in your browser or other apps, which have their own privacy practices.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences and does not knowingly collect information from anyone.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with an app update. Contact details for questions are on the app's Google Play listing.
