# Criczy privacy policy

_Last updated: 18 November 2026_

## Summary

Criczy never asks for an email address, phone number or password, and contains no advertising or analytics software. If you join the fan community, the username you choose and what you post are stored with Google Firebase, and posts are deleted after 24 hours.

## What stays on your device

Your favourites, settings, the list of players you have come across, a cache of scores so the app works offline, community posts you have already seen and the people you have blocked. These never leave your device, and are removed when you clear the app's storage or uninstall it.

## What is sent over the internet

To show scores, news and images the app requests them from cricket.com.au's servers. As with any website, those servers receive your IP address and basic technical details of the request. Criczy adds no identifiers to these requests.

## Fan community

Joining the community creates an anonymous account with Google Firebase, with no email, phone number or password, and stores the username you choose so nobody else can take it. Your posts, polls, votes and reports are stored in Firebase with that username, can be read by everyone using the app, and are deleted automatically 24 hours after the post was made. Opening a team's community downloads that team's posts. Firebase receives your IP address to provide the service. Report anything abusive from the post's menu; posts reported by several people are hidden.

## Notifications

Match alerts are worked out on your device by checking scores periodically. No notification service or token is involved. You can turn alerts off in More, or in your system settings.

## Links

News stories, videos, tickets and maps open in your browser or other apps, which have their own privacy practices.

## Children

The app is suitable for general audiences. The fan community is not directed at children under 13.

## Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published with an app update. Contact details for questions are on the app's Google Play listing.
