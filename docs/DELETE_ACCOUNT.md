# Delete your Criczy community account

_Criczy · cricket scores and fan community_

## In the app

1. Open **Favourites → Community**.
2. Tap **⋮** next to the refresh button.
3. Tap **Delete my community account**, then **Delete**.

## What is deleted

Your username, your profile, your posts and your anonymous Firebase account are deleted straight away. Votes and reports carry no name and are deleted automatically within 24 hours, with the posts they belong to.

Scores, favourites and settings stay on your phone; uninstalling the app removes them.

## Without the app

Email the support address below from any email account, with your username, and the account is deleted within 7 days.
